How to progress

Warm up before heavy lifts. Keep about 1–3 reps in reserve on most sets. When all working sets reach the top of the rep range with good form, add a small amount of weight next time. Rest 2–3 minutes on squats, hip thrusts and RDLs; 60–90 seconds on smaller exercises. Thursday is moderate conditioning; Saturday is optional if recovery is poor.
